Risk-Aware Optimization

Intelligent portfolio management that understands and respects your risk tolerance while maximizing returns.

What is Risk-Aware Optimization?

Risk-aware optimisation is the core of RAPOS. You pick a risk profile (Conservative, Moderate or Aggressive) and RAPOS uses Markowitz mean-variance optimisation to find the asset mix with the highest expected return for that profile's volatility target. It works from each asset's historical returns, volatility and correlations; the method is standard and the code is open on GitHub.

Key Benefits

Data-Driven Analysis

Estimates expected returns, volatility and correlations from historical prices, then solves for the best allocation for your risk profile.

Risk Balancing

Shows how far your current holdings are from the target mix and which trades would close the gap. You decide whether to make them.

Efficient Frontier

Plots every optimal risk/return combination and marks the minimum-risk and maximum-Sharpe portfolios.

Risk Breakdown

Shows how much each asset contributes to total portfolio risk, so concentration is easy to spot.

How It Works

1

Choose a Risk Profile

Pick Conservative, Moderate or Aggressive. Each one sets a target annual volatility (5%, 10% or 18%).

2

Portfolio Analysis

RAPOS estimates each asset's expected return and the covariance between assets from historical prices.

3

Optimization

A constrained optimiser (SciPy) finds the weights that maximise expected return within the profile's volatility target.
